To My Tanjeena 💖

Meri pyari Tanjeena, 💖✨

Har ek din mai tumse aur zyada mohabbat karne lagta hoon. Tum meri poori duniya ho, meri sabse pasandida insan aur meri zindagi ki sabse haseen roshni. 🌸💫

Mujhe tumhari hansi, tumhara masoom dil aur tumhara mere sath hona sabse zyada khushi deta hai. 🥺❤️

Jab bhi mai tumhara hath pakadta hoon ya tumhe gale lagata hoon, mujhe lagta hai duniya ki har tension ek pal ke liye ruk gayi hai. 🫂✨

Tumhara har touch, har smile, har choti si baat mere liye bohot precious hai. 🌹💖

Mera poora dil sirf aur sirf tumhara hai, aaj bhi, kal bhi, hamesha bhi. 👑❤️

I love you so much Tanjeena 💋🕊️

“If forever had a face, it would look like you.”
— Always yours 💖
